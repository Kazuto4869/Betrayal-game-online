## 2. Monster Turn & Companion Lifecycle (2E Rules Engine Integration)

- **Dog Companion**: Fully autonomous command flow (`COMMAND_DOG`). Traverses up to 6 spaces via doors and two-way stairs, ignores room barriers, cannot discover new rooms. Can transport 1 eligible item or fetch 1 dropped item back to owner. Once per turn limit with deterministic turn reset.
- **Monster Phase**:
  - Traitor explorer turn finishes -> Monster Phase begins if living monsters exist on the board.
  - Round order post-haunt: Living heroes clockwise from Traitor's left -> Traitor Explorer Turn -> Traitor-controlled Monster Phase -> next hero round.
  - If Traitor dies, Monster Phase still occurs at round wrap with the Traitor (or active haunt controlling seat) directing monsters.
  - Monsters roll Speed (minimum 1 guaranteed movement even on 0).
  - Leaving rooms containing opposing heroes costs +1 move per hero.
  - Monsters ignore room roll barriers (Chasm, Catacombs, Tower, Vault) and cannot discover new rooms.
  - Monsters can attack living heroes in their room once per monster turn. If a hero defeats a monster during the monster's turn, the monster is defended against but not stunned or harmed.
  - Stunned monsters clear their stun on their activation and conclude their turn without moving or attacking.
