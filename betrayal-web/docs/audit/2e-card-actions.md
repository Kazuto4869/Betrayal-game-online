## 5. Architectural Contract Derivation

Every held card maps to one primary use policy:

1. `passive`: Permanent equipment modifier while held in inventory; drops/transfers adjust derived stats immediately; never offers a `USE_ITEM` action.
2. `consumable`: Exactly one activation resolving prompts/rolls, followed by atomic movement from inventory/inPlay to discard.
3. `once_per_turn`: Exactly one activation per seat-turn; recorded in `player.usedCardsThisTurn`; resets at next `turn_started` for that seat.
4. `repeatable`: Gated by timing/target restrictions.
5. `manual`: Visibly flagged as requiring manual adjudication; rejected by engine `USE_ITEM`.
